Experience health and regeneration in the Thuringian Forest​

In a beautiful secluded location, surrounded by nature and forest, the Lifestylecenter Eyba is situated in a very charming landscape. Directly in the green heart of Germany, at the nature park Thuringian Forest, at 540m above sea level, south of the city of Saalfeld, you will find this quiet place. 

The valley offers excellent hiking routes and is a pleasant change to the busy daily life. Here you can relax from the noise of the city and experience stress-free times. The Gißrabach stream twists and turns along the property and provides for a unique fauna and flora. 

Wherever the eye looks, it enjoys the natural colours and green lush of the magnificent forest island. The restful peace and quiet as well as the fresh and pure forest breeze will refresh your heart! An ideal opportunity for you to recharge your energies and do something good for your health!
